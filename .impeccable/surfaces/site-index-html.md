# Surface brief — Oshare website + brand book

Scope: osharesushi.com prototype (home, menu, story, gallery, visit) and the brand book page. Visitor mode: **Persuade** (site), **Read** (brand book). Audience: Lowell locals deciding dinner on a phone; date/group nights; owners as first reviewers of a spec pitch. Action: order pickup on Toast, call, or walk in. Proof: real menu, real prices, real photos, real hours, aggregate ratings with sources. Constraints: no fabricated facts, chef bios, testimonials, cocktail list, parking; Toast is linked, never embedded.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The plate is the brand. Oshare's own cobalt blue-and-white porcelain and the orange brushed ensō become the whole identity: a porcelain-white ground, deep underglaze-cobalt fields, and one hot orange stroke. Refuses the dark-gold "luxury sushi" page and the zen-minimal cherry-blossom page.

OWN-WORLD: Glaze white #F3F5F9, underglaze cobalt #1F3F80 owning whole regions, cobalt wash #D9E3F2, blue-black ink #0B1A36, ensō orange #F25A0A as the only warm note. Shippori Mincho B1 (ink-pooled Japanese-foundry Latin) for display; Zen Kaku Gothic New for UI and menu. The double "rim rule" (heavy + hairline cobalt, like a bowl's painted rim) is the only divider. Traced vector ensō paints itself on.

STORY: Visitor sees real food on real porcelain, learns it's a chef-run sushi bar with a Korean-leaning kitchen whose rolls are named for Lowell, sees it's open now, and taps Order pickup.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Desktop: cobalt field left 5/12 with H1 "Dressed-up sushi on Market Street." in white mincho ~5.5rem, sub, Order pickup (orange) + See the menu; live open/closed line. Right 7/12 the marble flat-lay photo bleeding to the edge; the orange ensō, ~40vw, straddles the seam and paints itself on load. Sticky header with Order. Mobile: cobalt block with headline and ring, photo below, sticky bottom order bar.

FORM: Blue & White — sometsuke porcelain world; my top-ranked grounded candidate, chosen by the user on reroll 1 over the assigned "Oshare Magazine". Seed key c91c2fea (degraded roll, no challengers).

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

Signature interaction: the ensō brush ring paints itself (conic mask sweep) on load and draws around the active menu category. Motion grammar: one brush sweep, exponential ease-out, everything visible at rest; reduced-motion shows the finished ring.

Memorable moment: "The rolls Lowell named" — The Acre, Red Sox Maki, Celtic Roll set huge in mincho on cobalt.

Unresolved: cocktail list, interior/bar photography, chef surnames/bios, parking, lunch hours, social handles, photo license, naming intent of "Oshare".
